# Styles By So Fine

Fashion e-commerce and store-management platform built for **Styles By So Fine** — a Nigerian fashion business.

## About

A mobile-first online boutique plus a complete back-office, designed so the business can run day-to-day without a developer. The storefront is optimized for social-media traffic — fast, image-led, and simple to buy from on a phone — while the admin panel covers everything from stock to POS to homepage design.

## Features

**Storefront**
- Responsive catalogue with categories, collections, search and product pages supporting photos and video
- Product variants with UK sizing, stock tracking and pre-order support
- Wishlist, guest cart and a short one-page checkout
- Online card/bank payments; delivery fee is quoted separately after ordering and paid to the rider or store
- Order tracking, customer reviews, newsletter signup and a curated set of info pages (About, FAQ, Shipping, Returns & Exchanges, Size Guide, Privacy, Terms)
- SEO-ready: sitemap, search metadata, social share cards and structured product data

**Admin**
- Full product management: photos/videos, variants, options, pricing, stock and pre-orders
- Categories, collections, inventory movements, coupons and delivery zones
- Order management with fulfilment statuses and automatic customer emails
- Built-in POS for walk-in sales with offline payment methods and receipts
- Homepage designer: switchable presets, reorderable sections and a configurable hero slider
- Content management: pages, navigation menus and site-wide settings
- Staff accounts, sales/channel analytics, reviews, contact messages and an audit log
- Demo-data tools for previewing the store with sample content

**Trust & safety**
- Branded transactional emails: order confirmation, status updates, owner alerts and newsletter welcome
- Rate limiting on login and public forms, security headers, and signed payment webhooks
- Privacy-conscious: anonymous product view counts, newsletter unsubscribe links, cookie notice

## Media guidelines

Recommended upload sizes — hero slides 1920×1280, product photos 1200×1600, category tiles 800×1000, editorial images 900×1100 (WebP or JPG, roughly 200–400 KB). Product videos MP4/WebM, kept short and compressed.
